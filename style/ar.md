# Arabic (ar)

- **Modern Standard Arabic**, no dialect. Address the user in the masculine-neutral
  imperative common in software (*سجّل الدخول*, *اختر ملفًا شخصيًا*) unless a gender-neutral
  noun phrase reads naturally.
- Buttons use verbal nouns or short imperatives: *إلغاء*, *حفظ*, *إعادة المحاولة*.
- The text is laid out right to left. Keep Latin product names and placeholders as they are;
  don't add bidi control characters.
- Western digits come from the app's number formatting; don't convert digits by hand.
- Plurals: all six categories `zero one two few many other`. Use the dual for `two`
  (*موسمان*) and the counted-noun agreement Arabic needs in `few` (3–10) and `many` (11–99).
