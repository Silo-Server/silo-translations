# Japanese (ja)

- Polite **です/ます** style for sentences and messages. Buttons and menu items are short
  noun or verb forms without です/ます: *キャンセル*, *保存*, *再試行*.
- No spaces between Japanese and Latin text unless the English has a product name that reads
  better spaced (*Apple TV で再生*).
- Use full-width punctuation 、。「」 and the ellipsis ….
- Katakana for established loanwords (*サーバー*, *プロフィール*); keep glossary renderings.
- Plurals: only `other`; write the counter word (*#シーズン*, *#件*).
